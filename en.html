01Why now

Shipping the agent was the start. Making it better is the real cost.

  • Tuning takes people

    Results drift, tools fail, and experts and engineers go back to diagnose, patch and test. Every fix spends your most expensive people.

  • Lessons don’t stick

    A correction never becomes a rule or skill you can verify. On the next task, the same kind of problem shows up again.

  • Models keep changing

    Underlying models are updated constantly and behavior shifts quietly. Without regression checks, you can’t tell what broke.

  • Data has boundaries

    Documents, systems and models are bound by your environment. The improvement work has to stay inside what is authorized, too.

Evaluation tools tell you what went wrong.
MA fixes it, and proves the fix.

02How it works

One real improvement, start to finish.

On November 4, 2026, Frontis’s own Product Expert agent went through one evolution on MA. These are the six steps of that run, with figures from the run record.

  1. 01GOAL

    Define “good” first.

    The user provides a requirements document and an evaluation set. MA turns them into a quality guide, and only after the user confirms it does MA build cases, develop and evaluate.

    Of 26 cases, 24 pass every assertion. Along the way a scoring implementation issue surfaced. MA fixed it and rebuilt the baseline, and the old scores are not used to compute gains.

  3. 03ATTRIBUTION

    Turn failures into testable problems.

    Two real gaps are located. Each is tied to an assertion that can be re-tested, instead of a pile of logs patched one at a time.

    E21

  5. 05RETEST

    Measure every candidate with the same ruler.

    The middle candidates fixed the old problems but introduced new regressions, and were dropped. Candidate 3 completed a full re-evaluation: 26/26, with the average score going from 98.08 to 100.

    People decide adoption. Release is separate.

Sometimes nothing changes

Stop when the bar is met. Invalid runs don’t count.

In another run, the Financial Research agent’s valid baseline already met the bar on 7 of 7 cases. With the fixed goal satisfied, MA generated no candidates and changed nothing. The first evaluation had come back invalid on all 7 cases because the scoring environment never received the agent’s trajectory. That was not a score of zero, and the scores after the fix are not counted as improvement.

We already use Dify, HiAgent or our own agents. Why add MA?

Your business agent does the task. MA works out how to do the task better. Your existing platform stays. What can be connected and modified depends on the framework, its interfaces and your environment, so we start with a fit assessment.

Will MA change and release production versions on its own?

No. Finished research produces candidates and a report. Adoption into the dev package needs a person’s confirmation, and release is executed separately by someone with permission. Automatic research also stays within the agreed authorization and budget.

Does a local appliance mean data never leaves?

Not by location alone. Model calls, tools and data storage all have to run inside your domain, and the actual data flows have to be verified. We accept standalone deployment and data-stays-inside as two separate checks.

Can you guarantee an improvement?

Not in advance. Results depend on the task, the assets and the data. We agree on samples, metrics, budget and cost limits first, then let one validation answer the question.

Evidence and definitions

Every numbered figure on this page maps to a source below. Measured, target and self-reported figures are labeled as such.

  1. Self-reported · paper

    Medal average on MLE-Bench Lite for Frontis-MA1 (35B): 71.21% with OpenMLE-Evo-Max. Under the same OpenMLE-Evo framework, the base model scores 39.39% and Frontis-MA1 60.61%. Conditions: one RTX 4090 capped at 12 GB VRAM, 12 hours per task. Source: arXiv:2607.28568, July 30, 2026. Not yet independently replicated.

  2. Measured · internal run record

    Evolution run of the Product Expert agent (a Frontis internal agent) on November 4, 2026. Under the contract’s criterion of all 4/4 assertions passing, 24 of 26 cases rose to 26 of 26. Average score 98.08 to 100. 2 improved, 0 regressed. Under the quality guide’s lower per-case threshold, both versions score 26/26. Case positions for candidates 1 and 2 in the matrix are illustrative.

  3. Measured

    Grain price model research: 3 price targets, 5 candidate attempts. Model testing, prediction, attribution and reporting took 17 minutes 55 seconds, excluding data preparation. This is not a full delivery cycle. Recorded MAPE was 3.82%, against roughly 3.8% for the human-built model.

  4. Public

    Frontis-MA1-35B is published on Hugging Face. OpenRSI and OpenMLE are published on GitHub. Terms of use follow each repository’s license.

  5. Measured · internal run record

    Evaluation of the Financial Research agent, November 3–4, 2026: fixed baseline 7/7 valid and 7/7 meeting the bar, average score 0.998457. No evolution candidates were generated, and the agent was not modified or released. The first evaluation’s 7 invalid cases were caused by the scoring environment not receiving the agent’s trajectory. The agent under test, the cases, the weights and the pass line were unchanged.

  6. Measured

    Basis model research: accuracy rose from 57.28% for an early candidate to 62.14%, a gain of 4.86 percentage points. The human-built model scores 63%, which has not been exceeded.

  7. Self-reported · paper

    NatureBench Lite was held out from training. With the framework fixed, swapping in Frontis-MA1 raises Match-SOTA from 50% to 70%. Same source as note 1.

  8. Public

    BOM Agent 1.0 is in use at an integrated-circuit packaging-and-test customer, reconciling system ledgers, physical counts and actual consumption. The customer is not named.

    The category comparison summarizes public positioning to show where MA sits. It does not refer to any specific product.
